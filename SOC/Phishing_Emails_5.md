# Phishing Emails 5 – TryHackMe

## Overview
Completed the **Phishing Emails 5** room on TryHackMe, a hands-on lab that focuses on advanced phishing detection techniques, email header analysis, and recognizing malicious social engineering patterns.

## Key Concepts Learned
- How to analyze email headers for anomalies  
- Identification of spoofed senders and suspicious domains  
- Detection of malicious embedded links and attachments  
- Understanding advanced social engineering indicators  
- Correlation between phishing indicators and attack tactics

## SOC Relevance
Phishing is one of the most common initial access techniques in security incidents. This lab strengthens:
- Email threat detection capabilities  
- Incident response escalation processes  
- SOC analyst critical thinking  
- Forensic analysis of phishing campaigns
