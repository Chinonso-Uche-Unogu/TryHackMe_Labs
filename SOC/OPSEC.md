\# OPSEC (Operational Security)



\## Overview



Completed the TryHackMe OPSEC room, learning how Operational Security (OPSEC) helps protect critical information during red team engagements by reducing the risk of exposing activities, capabilities, and intentions.



\## Key Activities



\- Identified critical information requiring protection

\- Analysed potential threats and adversaries

\- Assessed OPSEC vulnerabilities

\- Evaluated operational risks

\- Applied countermeasures to reduce information exposure

\- Explored practical OPSEC scenarios in red team operations

\- Threat Analysis

\- Red Team Operations

\## Key Takeaway



Developed a practical understanding of the five-step OPSEC process and how it helps protect sensitive information, reduce operational risk, and improve the effectiveness of red team engagements.
