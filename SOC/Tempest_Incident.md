\# Tempest Incident



\## Overview



Completed a hands-on incident response investigation involving the analysis of security logs, attacker activity, and indicators of compromise to determine the scope and impact of a security incident.



\## Key Activities



\- Investigated security alerts and suspicious events

\- Analysed attacker behaviour across multiple log sources

\- Correlated events to reconstruct the attack timeline

\- Identified indicators of compromise (IOCs)

\- Investigated persistence and attacker techniques

\- Documented findings and incident evidence



\## Tools \& Concepts



\- Incident Response

\- Threat Hunting

\- Log Analysis

\- Event Correlation

\- IOC Analysis

\- Digital Forensics

\- MITRE ATT\&CK

\- Security Investigation



\## Key Takeaway



Practised investigating a real-world incident by analysing evidence, correlating security events, identifying attacker techniques, and documenting findings to support incident response.
