\# Red Team Threat Intel



\## Overview



Completed the TryHackMe Red Team Threat Intel room, learning how Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) supports adversary emulation and red team planning using real-world threat intelligence.



\## Key Activities



\- Explored Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) concepts

\- Used the MITRE ATT\&CK framework to analyse adversary TTPs

\- Investigated Carbanak threat actor techniques

\- Mapped attacker behaviour to the Cyber Kill Chain

\- Identified indicators of compromise (IOCs)

\- Applied threat intelligence to support red team planning



\## Tools \& Concepts



\- Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI)

\- MITRE ATT\&CK

\- Cyber Kill Chain

\- Adversary Emulation

\- Threat Actor Profiling

\- IOC Analysis

\- TTP Mapping

\- Red Team Planning



\## Key Takeaway



Developed a practical understanding of how threat intelligence can be used to analyse adversaries, map attack techniques, and support realistic red team engagements.
