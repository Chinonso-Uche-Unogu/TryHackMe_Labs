\# Shadow Trace – TryHackMe



\## Overview

Completed the Shadow Trace room on TryHackMe. This lab focused on digital forensics and incident investigation, tracing attacker activity through system artifacts, logs, and evidence left behind after a compromise.



\## Key Concepts Learned

\- Digital forensics fundamentals

\- Incident investigation techniques

\- Timeline analysis

\- Evidence collection and correlation

\- Indicators of Compromise (IOCs)

\- Attacker activity reconstruction



\## Tools \& Technologies

\- Log analysis

\- Windows artifacts

\- Forensic investigation techniques

\- Threat hunting concepts



\## SOC Relevance

Digital forensics and evidence analysis help SOC analysts understand attacker behavior, determine the scope of incidents, and support effective incident response.
